# Public dataset access and feasibility audit

**Audit date:** 2026-10-25  
**Status:** documentary triage. Not all downloadable archives/licenses have been inspected locally. None of the restricted or licensed corpora is approved for model use until exact versions, access terms, labels, participant IDs and metadata are verified.

## Summary

“Publicly described” does not necessarily mean “freely downloadable.” Several important language-diverse speech corpora require an access request, a data-use agreement or institutional approval. Published papers also use different filtered subsets of a corpus. We must report the independent participant count remaining after every filter, not merely the number of audio files or windows.

## Feasibility conclusions

1. **Language-diverse raw audio is the main bottleneck.** EWA-DB requires request/EULA; NeuroVoz is restricted; PC-GITA/ParkCeleb generally require requests or institutional access; Bridge2AI raw audio requires institutional sign-off.
2. **MDVR-KCL and ItalianPVS appear to be the most accessible raw-audio candidates**, but sample sizes are small. They alone cannot power reliable language-by-sex interaction tests.
3. **Feature datasets are not substitutes for raw multilingual audio.** UCI 301/489 and Oxford are suitable for demonstrating leakage prevention and reproducing classic baselines, but cannot establish cross-language raw-audio generalization.
4. **Do not pool all corpora at first sight.** Harmonize diagnosis definitions, controls, tasks, medication state, demographics, devices, participant identifiers and licenses; count distinct eligible participants per task/class.
5. **Do not redistribute licensed audio.** If we use licensed corpora, release code, permitted aggregate results and reconstruction instructions rather than the recordings.
6. Dataset counts in the primary papers can differ from the source-corpus counts. Record the exact version and exclusion path for every dataset.

## Dataset approval checklist

For every proposed corpus, record:
- DOI/URL, version and access date;
- license/EULA and publication/redistribution permissions;
- participant IDs and repeat-recording mapping;
- PD diagnosis source and definition;
- control definition and exclusion of other neurodegenerative diagnoses;
- stage, medication state and relevant clinical labels;
- language, speech task, equipment/site and recording conditions;
- age, sex and missing demographics by diagnosis;
- raw audio versus derived feature/transcript availability;
- number of unique participants and recordings after every exclusion;
- overlap with other corpora or previously published training/test sets;
- whether the exact model inputs and preprocessing can be reproduced.

**Approval rule:** no dataset enters the primary analysis until these items are documented. If fewer than two genuinely independent, compatible datasets remain, pivot to a focused registered review or single-dataset methodological replication rather than claiming cross-language clinical validation.
