## Cross-cutting coding notes

- Keep the unit of analysis explicit: person, visit/session, recording, clip, overlapping window, or feature row.
- For repeated recordings, group participants before any train/validation/test split, including folds used for feature selection and calibration.
- Record whether a reported external cohort is fully independent of any pretraining data or derived-feature training set.
- Separate ranking metrics (AUROC), threshold-dependent metrics (sensitivity/specificity/F1), probability metrics (Brier/calibration slope), and clinical utility.
- Compare subgroup performance only with participant counts and uncertainty.
- “Language” is not a causal explanatory variable when language is perfectly confounded with site/task/device/cohort; describe combined domain shift instead.
